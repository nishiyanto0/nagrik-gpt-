The Challenge

Current complaint systems are slow and frustrating. Heavy reliance on manual logs causes delays, and citizens often have no way to track their reports. This lack of transparency leaves issues unresolved and damages trust in local governance.

💡

The Solution

NagrikGPT connects citizens, administrators, and field officers on one unified platform. It simplifies the workflow, ensuring that complaints are not just heard but actually acted upon with full transparency and accountability.

📈 The Impact

By cutting delays and making the process visible, the platform significantly speeds up resolution times. NSS camps from colleges can easily find issues near them without extensive research. It restores accountability and helps rebuild trust between the community and local authorities.
